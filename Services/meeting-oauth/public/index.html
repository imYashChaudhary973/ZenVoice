ZenVoice is a local-first macOS app for dictation and bot-free meeting notes. Record with consent, transcribe on your Mac, and keep your notes in local encrypted storage.

View the projectRead the privacy policy

Meeting integrations are under development. Google authorization is not yet verified for unrestricted public access; availability depends on your build and Google approval.

Context you choose to connect.

Connections are optional and read-only. ZenVoice uses them to prepare a brief for an upcoming meeting—not to send email, change your calendar, or join your call.

Google Calendar
Choose calendars, then read their upcoming events, meeting links, and invitees. Calendar access does not start a recording.
Gmail
Find recent messages related to a meeting’s invitees and include relevant snippets in your preparation. ZenVoice requests read-only mail access; it cannot send or delete email.
Slack
Choose joined channels and use recent messages as meeting context. No bot or posting permissions are requested.

Connected does not mean uploaded by default.

Calendar and message content is fetched directly by the Mac app. The authorization service exchanges codes and tokens, not your messages or recordings. Briefs use on-device Apple Intelligence by default; sharing preparation text with your configured text provider requires a separate opt-in.

Disconnect an account in ZenVoice to remove its local tokens and cached source content. You can also revoke Google access from your Google Account connections.
