Privacy policy

Effective September 13, 2026

ZenVoice is developed by Yash Chaudhary. This policy covers the ZenVoice macOS app, its optional account connections, and this website and authorization service. Features vary by build. Google verification for public use is pending; this policy does not imply approval by Google.

For privacy questions or help deleting data, email imyash.chaudhary2@gmail.com. Do not include private messages, recordings, passwords, or access tokens in support requests.

Google and Slack data we access

Accounts connect only after you initiate sign-in and grant permission. You can use dictation and local meeting recording without connecting Google or Slack.

  • Google Calendar: read-only access to your calendar list lets you choose calendars. Read-only event access supplies upcoming event titles, times, descriptions, meeting links, and invitee details from those selected calendars.
  • Gmail: read-only mail access lets the Mac app search for recent messages associated with meeting invitees and obtain message metadata and snippets. Preparation is bounded to up to ten recent snippets for up to ten invitees. The granted scope can read mail broadly, but ZenVoice uses it for this meeting-preparation feature. It does not send, modify, or delete mail.
  • Slack: read-only channel-list and history permissions discover joined public and private channels you can access. Preparation reads up to fifteen recent messages from each explicitly selected channel, for up to three channels. No bot, posting permission, or workspace-wide message search is used.
  • Account information: account identifiers, email addresses or workspace names identify the connection in the app. Access and refresh tokens maintain the connection until expiry or revocation.

We use this information to show your upcoming meetings, let you select sources, and create source-backed meeting preparation. Invitees are not verified attendees. Connecting a calendar never starts a recording, invites anyone, or joins a call.

Where information is processed

The Mac app fetches calendar events and message content directly from Google or Slack over HTTPS. The ZenVoice authorization service hosted on Render handles authorization codes and access/refresh-token exchange. Tokens pass through that service in memory; it does not fetch or store your calendar, email, Slack message, transcript, or audio content.

Pending authorization flows expire after ten minutes and handoff tickets after one minute. Expired records are periodically removed from service memory. The app stores connected-account tokens in the macOS Keychain. The service keeps provider application credentials in its server environment, not in the app.

Source excerpts, event metadata, meeting transcripts, and generated briefs retained by ZenVoice are encrypted in local meeting storage with a Keychain-protected key. They are decrypted in memory when used. Device backups, files you export, and other software with access to your unlocked Mac are outside this protection.

AI processing and third-party sharing

Meeting preparation uses on-device Apple Intelligence by default. There is no automatic cloud fallback. A separate, off-by-default setting allows selected preparation text—including connected-source excerpts—to be sent directly from your Mac to the text provider you configure, using your own credentials. Audio is not part of that request.

Before enabling that setting, review your provider’s retention and data-use terms. ZenVoice cannot control an independently configured provider’s practices. Leave it off to keep connected-source text out of that provider. The current configurable-provider path requires compliance review before unrestricted Google rollout.

ZenVoice does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google-derived data is used for the user-facing features described here. ZenVoice’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This commitment is not a claim that Google has completed its review.

We do not operate a server-side repository of your message content for staff to read. If you voluntarily send information to support, we use it to respond to your request. Public-research queries are sent to Wikipedia only when you explicitly enter them; meeting titles and private text are not automatically sent to public search.

Retention, disconnecting, and deletion

  • Upcoming-event caches age out. Source snapshots attached to meetings remain until source disconnect or meeting deletion.
  • Use the account’s Disconnect control in ZenVoice to remove its locally stored tokens and cached source content and invalidate derived briefs, including connected-source content in prior meeting snapshots.
  • Revoke Google authorization independently at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; use ZenVoice’s disconnect/delete controls to remove data already stored on your Mac.
  • Delete a meeting in ZenVoice to remove its local transcript and audio. Clearing meeting records is separate from disconnecting account sources.
  • Exports and copied text are unencrypted, deliberate user actions. Deleting data in ZenVoice cannot remove copies you exported, shared, backed up, or sent to a third-party provider. Manage those copies with their recipient or provider.

Dictation and recording

Local speech engines are the default. Meeting recording requires an explicit action and consent acknowledgment; it does not use a meeting bot. Microphone and application/system audio are independent of a call app’s mute setting. Pause or stop ZenVoice to exclude private audio.

Meeting audio and transcripts are encrypted locally. Completed meeting audio expires after seven days, checked at launch and completion; interrupted audio remains for recovery until you delete the meeting. Transcripts remain until deletion.

Dictation transcripts are encrypted locally and may be copied to the system clipboard for insertion. Optional dictation Audio History is off by default and stores unencrypted audio protected by local file permissions and configurable size/age limits. Failed-dictation recovery may retain audio for up to 24 hours when enabled.

Optional cloud speech uploads a dictation clip to your selected provider after you opt in. Optional cloud formatting sends finished text and your prompt. Both use your credentials and are separate from local meeting transcription. Approved agentic actions can run external tools with their own accounts and network behavior. Review the action before approving it.

Website, hosting, and downloads

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or application cookies. Render processes the network and operational information necessary to serve requests, such as IP addresses and request metadata, under its privacy policy. Application error responses do not echo authorization codes or tokens. Hosting infrastructure may maintain operational logs; do not put private content in support URLs.

Model downloads and update checks contact their hosting services. They do not upload your recordings or transcripts. Google, Slack, hosting providers, and any text/speech provider you select also operate under their own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when these practices change and revise the effective date. New permissions or new optional cloud processing require the corresponding consent or opt-in. Contact imyash.chaudhary2@gmail.com with questions about this policy, account access, or local deletion.
